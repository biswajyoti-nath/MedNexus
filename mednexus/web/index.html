GOVERNANCE · SOURCE REVIEW · 04 NOV 2026

Research safeguards

Trust comes from evidence, clear limits, and accountable review—not from a badge. This page separates what the demo shows from the work required before any clinical use.

i
Research prototype · readiness not established

This interface makes no claim of regulatory approval, legal compliance, clinical validation, or suitability for patient care. Applicability depends on intended purpose, users, data flows, deployment, and jurisdiction.

VISIBLE IN THIS DEMO

Safeguards and their limits

01

Clear intended scope

NeuroVision is presented as a 2D research-slice screen. Its score is not a calibrated clinical probability or a patient-level diagnosis.

Scope disclosed in the UI
02

Source and model visibility

Review records show the submitted source, route, model provenance, and session time where available. Keep the original scan or report in view.

Session-level provenance
03

Human review

Outputs are described as preliminary and direct users to a qualified clinician. This demo does not verify reviewer credentials or provide a clinical sign-off workflow.

No clinical oversight workflow
04

Explainability views

Grad-CAM, agreement, and uncertainty maps are exploratory visualizations. They are not validated explanations, causal evidence, or proof that a region is a lesion.

Interpretability not clinically validated
05

Temporary file handling

The browser session is not a patient record. MRI inference routes through configured local services and its temporary upload is cleaned up; verify service endpoints, logs, and retention before any sensitive-data use.

Local setup ≠ privacy certification
06

Performance evidence

This app does not present external clinical validation, subgroup performance, scanner/site generalization, prospective outcomes, or post-release monitoring evidence.

Evidence not established here
BEFORE A CLINICAL OR PATIENT-FACING PILOT

Evidence and governance gates

These are readiness questions, not completed controls or a substitute for expert review.

01

Define the intended use

Name the user, patient population, care setting, clinical purpose, inputs, outputs, exclusions, and supported scanners/sites. Get a jurisdiction-specific classification review.

To establish
02

Validate on representative data

Pre-register endpoints; evaluate an independent, appropriately governed cohort; report uncertainty, failures, calibration, subgroup results, and limits across sites and scanners.

To establish
03

Test the human–AI workflow

Study usability and automation bias with intended users. Keep original material available, define escalation and override paths, and document who is accountable for decisions.

To establish
04

Govern data and security

Map every data flow, endpoint, log, temporary file, backup, and vendor. Define lawful basis/consent as applicable, access control, retention/deletion, incident response, and security testing.

To establish
05

Control versions and monitor

Record model, weights, preprocessing, and interface versions. Set change review, drift monitoring, complaint/adverse-event handling, rollback, and periodic revalidation.

To establish
06

Set research ethics oversight

Determine whether ethics committee review, participant information/consent, data-use permissions, and institutional approvals are required before using real health data.

To establish
PRIMARY-SOURCE LANDSCAPE

Frameworks to assess

These references are conditional and jurisdiction-specific. Listing them here does not mean the prototype is subject to, or compliant with, each one.

INDIA · MEDICAL SOFTWARE

CDSCO · Medical Devices Rules

CDSCO says device status turns on intended purpose and lists a Medical Device Software guidance document under MDR-2017 dated 21 July 2026. Assess the software’s purpose and route with Indian regulatory counsel.

CDSCO medical device guidance ↗
INDIA · PERSONAL DATA

DPDP Act and Rules

The 2025 Act and Rules use phased commencement. Confirm which provisions apply on the intended launch date and to the actual controller, processor, and data flow; do not treat local hosting as an exemption.

MeitY · Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 ↗
UNITED STATES · SOFTWARE FUNCTION

FDA · Clinical Decision Support

FDA’s non-device CDS criteria exclude software that analyzes medical images such as MRI. Do not assume that this exclusion covers NeuroVision; intended use and the full regulatory context still need expert review.

FDA · Clinical Decision Support Software ↗
UNITED STATES · DATA RELATIONSHIP

HHS · HIPAA scope

HIPAA applies to covered entities and business associates as defined by the relationship and activity; it does not automatically apply to every health app. Map provider/vendor access and agreements.

HHS · Covered Entities and Business Associates ↗
EUROPEAN UNION · AI AND MEDICAL DEVICE LAW

EU AI Act · classification matters

Medical-purpose AI may be high-risk where the Act’s medical-device conditions are met. The Commission’s current timeline distinguishes Annex III and regulated-product systems; classification and obligations need a specific assessment.

European Commission · AI Act FAQ and timeline ↗
INTERNATIONAL · RESEARCH ETHICS

WHO · AI-related health research

WHO’s September 2026 report calls for stronger ethics oversight across the research lifecycle, including attention to transparency, bias, fairness, accountability, privacy, and potential harms.

WHO · Ethics review and oversight for AI health research ↗
